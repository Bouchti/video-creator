# YPP Eligibility Rules and Real Shorts Revenue (as of 7 Nov 2026)

Context: new channel, one original 60-70 s vertical cartoon 3D history/science Short per day (AI TTS, code-animated, burned-in subs), goal = monetized within about one month.

**Main finding first:** On 10 Aug 2026 YouTube announced YPP changes that **take effect 1 Feb 2027**. Until 31 Jan 2027 the old bars still apply: 1,000 subs plus either 4,000 hours or 10M Shorts views in 90 days. From 1 Feb 2027, new applicants need **1,000 subs + 8,000 hours / 365 days OR 20M Shorts views / 90 days**. Separately, **every** partner, new or old, then needs a rolling **10M qualified Shorts views per 90 days** to receive any Shorts Creator Pool money. The 500-sub fan-funding tier is unchanged.

---

## Q1. Exact YPP thresholds, tiers, features, countries, and 2025-2026 changes

### Takeaway
Today (Nov 2026) the full ad-revenue tier is 1,000 subs plus either 4,000 qualified long-form watch hours in 12 months or 10M qualified Shorts views in 90 days. The 500-sub tier is 500 subs, 3 public uploads in 90 days, and either 3,000 hours or 3M Shorts views. It unlocks fan funding and Shopping only, with **no ad revenue**. From 1 Feb 2027 the full-tier entry bar doubles for new applicants, and a rolling 10M/90-day Shorts floor applies to everyone.

### Cited Findings
**Current full tier (in force until 31 Jan 2027)**
- Option 1: "1,000 subscribers with 4,000 qualified watch hours in the last 12 months." Option 2: "1,000 subscribers with 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days." Shorts-feed watch hours don't count toward the 4,000 hours. — [YouTube Help: YPP overview & eligibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851)
- It unlocks ad revenue sharing plus the modules Watch Page Ads, Shorts Feed Ads, Memberships and Supers. Each module needs its terms accepted. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851)
- Joining also requires: following the channel monetization policies, living in a YPP country, no active Community Guidelines strikes, 2-Step Verification on, advanced features access, and one active AdSense for YouTube account linked. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851)
- Ad revenue: "1,000 subscribers," plus either 4,000 qualified long-form watch hours in 365 days or 10M qualified Shorts views in 90 days. — [YouTube Help: How to earn money on YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857?hl=en)

**Lower / expanded / "fan funding" tier (unchanged by the 2026 announcement)**
- 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in the last 90 days, and either 3,000 long-form watch hours in 365 days or 3M Shorts views in 90 days. It unlocks fan funding and select Shopping features, **not ads or Premium revenue**. — [vidIQ, YPP changes 2027](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-partner-program-changes-2027/); [Tubefilter, 10 Aug 2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/08/10/youtube-partner-program-ad-eligibility-requirements-shorts/amp/)
- Per YouTube Help, channel memberships require the same 500 subs / 3 uploads / 3,000 hours or 3M Shorts views, plus age 18+ and a supported country. Super Chat, Super Thanks and Shopping have their own requirements. — [YouTube Help 72857](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857?hl=en)
- Fan funding covers memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks and YouTube Shopping for your own products. — [Social Media Today (2023-24 expansion)](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-expands-creator-monetization-options-more-regions/697840/)
- It also unlocks Creator Partnerships (brand deals) and Shopping, and the 2026 update does not change it. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-cuts-shorts-pay-for-channels-under-10m-views-from-february-2027/)

**Countries for the lower tier**
- It launched June 2023 in the US, UK, Canada, Taiwan and South Korea, then expanded to more countries, including MENA countries such as Saudi Arabia, Morocco, Algeria, Kuwait, Lebanon and Oman. Reports conflict on the count: one says "37 more countries … 99 regions in total," MediaPost says "33 additional countries." (2023-2024 data.) — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-expands-creator-monetization-options-more-regions/697840/); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/388739/youtube-expands-creator-monetization-access-launc.html); [Communicate Online](https://communicateonline.me/news/youtubes-latest-addition-to-its-partnerships-program-opens-doors-for-creators/)
- The authoritative list sits on YouTube Help answer 7101720 (YPP availability), linked from [72857](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857?hl=en). I could not retrieve it (see Gaps).

**2026 changes (announced 10 Aug 2026, effective 1 Feb 2027)**
- New applicants: 1,000 subs plus either 8,000 hours / 365 days or 20M Shorts views / 90 days. Existing partners are grandfathered on entry. — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/08/10/youtube-partner-program-ad-eligibility-requirements-shorts/amp/); [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-partner-program-changes-2027/)
- From Feb 2027, "only creators with at least 10 million Shorts views over 90 days can receive a share of Shorts ad revenue, regardless of how they qualified." Shorts ads switch back on automatically once a channel is back above the line. — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/08/10/youtube-partner-program-ad-eligibility-requirements-shorts/amp/)
- The official Shorts revenue page confirms: "at least 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days … starting February 1, 2027." — [YouTube Help: Shorts revenue sharing](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)
- The floor is re-evaluated monthly on a rolling 90 days. Channels below it stay in YPP. Long-form ads, memberships, Super Chat, Shopping affiliate and Creator Partnerships are unaffected. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-cuts-shorts-pay-for-channels-under-10m-views-from-february-2027/)
- New activity test for partners from 1 Feb 2027. A channel must meet one of:
  - 1,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days
  - 1M qualified Shorts views in 90 days
  - 2 long-form or 5 Shorts uploads every 90 days

  Channels that miss all three get a 90-day recovery window. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-partner-program-changes-2027/); [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-partner-program-changes-2027/)
- Current partners must accept the updated terms by 31 Jan 2027 or the affected earnings pause. — [YouTube Help 72851](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-cuts-shorts-pay-for-channels-under-10m-views-from-february-2027/)
- Terminology: "valid public" views and hours are renamed "qualified" views and watch hours. — [search summary of coverage, e.g. AIR](https://air.io/en/monetization/youtube-partner-program-requirements-2026-the-complete-guide)
- YouTube VP Amjad Hanif: the changes reflect "adjustments based on the amazing growth of the creator ecosystem." YouTube wants Shorts ads to be "meaningful." For channels under 10M it promises Shopping bonuses, brand-deal production credits and trend-based boosts, with no details yet. — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/08/10/youtube-partner-program-ad-eligibility-requirements-shorts/amp/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-cuts-shorts-pay-for-channels-under-10m-views-from-february-2027/)
- New from Feb 2027: Shorts ads targeted at five or fewer channels pay a direct 45% of net to the creator. — [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)
- Payout-threshold test: from Oct 2026 payments, some randomly selected partners in the US, India and Indonesia get paid at $20 instead of $100. — [OutlierKit (third party, unverified)](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-partner-program-changes-2027/)

### Inferences
- For a channel starting now, the only full-tier route that can work in about 30 days is the **Shorts path: 1,000 subs + 10M qualified Shorts views in 90 days**. A Shorts-only channel cannot earn the 4,000 hours, because Shorts-feed watch time doesn't count. With 30 Shorts that means about 333k engaged views per Short on average, or roughly 111k-333k engaged views a day depending on the window used.
- Timing matters a lot. If the channel crosses 10M before 1 Feb 2027 and its application is judged under the old rules, it gets in at 10M. If not, the bar becomes 20M per 90 days.
- After 1 Feb 2027, admission alone pays nothing from Shorts. Earning Shorts revenue needs at least 10M views per 90 days, about 3.33M a month, sustained.
- The 500-sub tier (3M Shorts views / 90 days) is the realistic first milestone. It pays no ad money: only memberships, Supers, Shopping and brand-deal access.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve the official blog.youtube announcement or the YPP country list (Help answer 7101720). Lower-tier country coverage as of late 2026 is unverified, and the 2023-24 reports conflict (33 vs 37 added countries).
- Whether an application **submitted or pending before 1 Feb 2027** is judged under the old 10M/4,000h rules is not officially stated. vidIQ says the announcement "does not clearly answer this transition case." OutlierKit claims applying before 1 Feb "should lock in current rules," but that is third-party opinion.
- Whether a channel already in the 500-sub tier can upgrade under the old bars after 1 Feb is unclear ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-partner-program-changes-2027/)).

## Q2. What counts as a "qualified / valid public Shorts view"

### Takeaway
Since 31 Mar 2025 the public Shorts view counter counts every start or replay. YPP eligibility and earnings still use the older "engaged views" metric, which is lower. Plan against engaged views in Analytics > Advanced Mode, not the public counter. Shorts watch time never counts toward the 4,000 or 8,000 hours.

### Cited Findings
- From 31 Mar 2025 a Shorts view is counted each time a Short starts or replays, with no minimum watch time. YouTube said this "will not affect YPP eligibility or earnings, as both will be based on 'engaged views'." The old metric is kept as "engaged views" in YouTube Analytics > Advanced Mode. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-changes-shorts-view-counts-no-change-to-monetization/543005/); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/404562/youtube-changes-how-it-counts-shorts-views.html); [Digital Music News, 27 Mar 2025](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2025/03/27/how-does-youtube-shorts-count-views-2025/)
- One vidIQ example: public Shorts views rose about 69% after the change while engaged views and revenue stayed flat. This is a single-channel anecdote. — [vidIQ video via Rosetta](https://rosetta.to/u/vidiq/this-is-what-happens-when-youtube-changes-view-counts)
- Shorts don't count toward the YPP watch-hour threshold. Neither do private, unlisted or deleted videos, or ad-campaign views. — [YouTube Help 72851](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851); [coverage summary, e.g. Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/150324/youtube-partner-program-minimum-qualified-public-watch-hours-shorts-views)
- These views earn no Creator Pool revenue:
  - non-original Shorts (unedited clips, compilations with nothing added)
  - artificial or bot views
  - views on content that breaks the advertiser-friendly guidelines
  - views before the Shorts Monetization Module is accepted

  — [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)

### Inferences
- The public view counter will overstate progress toward 10M. Track engaged views in Advanced Mode. The ratio is often about 0.6:1 (based on the 69% anecdote), but it varies by channel.
- Original, code-animated Shorts should count as "original." The AI TTS voice is a reviewer-judgment point under the inauthentic/repetitious content policy (see Q3).

### Gaps
- There is no official published ratio of engaged views to public views.

## Q3. How the YPP application review works

### Takeaway
Steps: meet the threshold, go to Studio > Earn > Apply, accept the Base terms, then link or create AdSense inside Studio (2-Step Verification must be on). The channel then joins a queue reviewed by automated systems plus humans. YouTube says decisions come "typically in about 1 month." After a rejection you can appeal within 21 days or reapply after 30 days, and after a repeat rejection the wait is 90 days.

### Cited Findings
- Process: Studio > Earn > "Apply Now" > accept Base terms > "Start" to set up or link AdSense, creating it only from inside Studio. The channel then goes into a review queue where "automated systems and human reviewers" check it as a whole. Applications are handled in order received, and a channel may need multiple reviews if reviewers disagree. — [YouTube Help 72851](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851)
- Timeline: "typically in about 1 month," with delays possible. — [YouTube Help 72851](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851)
- Third-party reports put it at 1-30 days or 1-4 weeks. AIR says its clients usually hear back within a week. — [vidIQ get monetized](https://www.vidiq.com/blog/post/get-monetized-youtube); [AIR](https://air.io/en/monetization/how-long-does-it-take-to-get-monetized-on-youtube); [vidIQ Shorts monetization](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-monetization/)
- Rejection: appeal within 21 days, or keep uploading and re-apply after 30 days. After a repeat rejection or re-application the wait is 90 days. — [YouTube Help 72851](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851)
- What reviewers look at, per third-party guides: the channel's main theme, its most-viewed videos and its newest videos. They also look at video titles, thumbnails and descriptions. Changing privacy settings or deleting videos during review can affect the application. — [Rhei help center summary](https://intercom.help/rhei/en/articles/9295357-youtube-channel-monetization-policies); [247livestream guide](https://247livestream.pages.dev/blog/youtube-partner-program-eligibility.html)
- After acceptance, Shorts monetization isn't automatic: you must enable the Shorts Monetization Module, and only views after acceptance earn. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-monetization/); [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)

### Inferences
- Realistic total time: if the threshold is hit around day 30, approval can land anywhere from about day 37 to about day 60+. "Monetized within one month" therefore needs the threshold reached in about 2-3 weeks.
- The reviewer risk for this format is the "inauthentic / mass-produced / repetitious content" policy (AI voice, templated look). Visible per-video originality (a different story and set each day, written narration) is the main protection. This is my inference from the policy names; I did not verify the policy text in this pass.

### Gaps
- I found no official confirmation that the About section is reviewed. Third-party lists name the theme, most-viewed and newest videos.
- The official rejection-reason taxonomy was not retrieved. Commonly cited reasons are reused content, repetitious content and inauthentic content, but I did not verify this list with a source here.

## Q4. Shorts revenue sharing mechanics and real RPMs

### Takeaway
Each month, Shorts-feed ad revenue in each country is pooled, minus a music cut for Shorts that use tracks. It is then split by each monetizing creator's share of engaged views, and the creator keeps 45%. Real Shorts RPMs cluster around **$0.03-$0.20 per 1,000 views**. The best dataset (AIR, 274 channels) has the US at **$0.33**. History and education Shorts sit at the low-to-middle end. Long-form education RPM runs about **$15-18**, roughly 50-150x more per view.

### Cited Findings
**Mechanics**
- Shorts-feed ad revenue is pooled monthly. Shorts with one music track send half their revenue to licensing, two tracks two-thirds, and Shorts with no music send all of it to the Creator Pool. The pool is allocated by share of engaged views from monetizing creators in each country. The creator keeps 45%, and Premium revenue for Shorts also pays 45%. — [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)
- YouTube's worked example: 100M engaged views, $100k revenue, 20% one-track usage → pool $90k → 1M views = 1% = $900 → $405 after the 45% share (an effective $0.405 RPM in a rich example market). — [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)
- Long-form split is 55% to the creator, Shorts 45%, both unchanged in 2026. — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-partner-program-changes-2027/)

**Reported RPMs (USD per 1,000 views, after YouTube's cut)**
- AIR Media-Tech, 274 channels, 13 niches, 3,044 channel-months, YouTube Analytics API, updated 16 Sep 2026:
  - Shorts RPM by country: **US $0.328, Australia $0.193, UK $0.166, Canada $0.165, Germany $0.163**
  - Most niches $0.07-$0.20 on Shorts
  - Shorts RPM is "3-14% of long-form RPM in almost every niche"
  - Education long-form RPM **$14.97-$18.23**, but with no education Shorts channels in the sample
  - Entertainment channels' long-form RPM fell from $4.99 to $1.15 as Shorts volume rose to 21+ a month (correlational)

  — [AIR data findings](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-shorts-rpm-vs-long-form-how-much-do-shorts-earn-in-2026)
- vidIQ:
  - Typical Shorts RPM $0.03-$0.10, and real channels fall in that band "roughly 80% of the time"
  - vidIQ's own channel moved from about $0.035 (468.5k views = $16.61) to about $0.10
  - Q4 peaks, and January is noticeably lower

  — [vidIQ Shorts monetization 2026](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-monetization/). A vidIQ video says its 2025-26 average Shorts RPM was about $0.13. — [Rosetta/vidIQ](https://rosetta.to/u/vidiq/how-much-youtube-actually-pays-for-shorts-in-2026)
- History Shorts estimates, from SEO and vendor blogs with weak methodology:
  - Fluxnote: $0.04-$0.10 — [Fluxnote history](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-shorts-rpm-history-niche)
  - Satura planning range: $0.10-$0.50, base $0.25, US-heavy and original — [Satura](https://saturaai.com/history-military-youtube-shorts-rpm)
- Education Shorts estimates: $0.04-$0.15 — [Fluxnote education](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-shorts-rpm-education-niche)
- r/PartneredYoutube had creators complaining about low Shorts RPM and a January 2026 slump. These are anecdotes from a mirror, with no numbers extracted. — [Reddit mirror snapshot](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/subreddits/PartneredYoutube/snapshots/2026-01-10T03%3A01%3A14.011615Z)

### Inferences
- Planning RPMs for an English history/science cartoon Short channel:
  - Low: $0.04 (global mixed audience)
  - Base: $0.10 (mixed, with US/UK at about 30-40%)
  - High: $0.25-$0.33 (US-dominant, Q4)
- Not using copyrighted music keeps 100% of the Short's revenue in the pool. Synthesized or original music therefore helps slightly. It doesn't change the 45% share, but it raises the pool your views draw from.

### Gaps
- No rigorous history-specific Shorts RPM dataset was found. The AIR niche tables are images I couldn't read, and the history figures come from low-quality blogs.
- No long-form RPM specific to history was found. Only AIR's "education" category ($14.97-$18.23) is available.

## Q5. Worked income math (monthly, engaged views, after YouTube's cut)

### Takeaway
Shorts ad income is small. 1M views a month is about $30-$330, 10M about $300-$3,300, and 30M about $0.9k-$10k, with about $100 / $1k / $3k as base cases.

### Cited Findings
- vidIQ table: 1M views = $30 / $60 / $100 and 10M views = $300 / $600 / $1,000 at $0.03 / $0.06 / $0.10. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-monetization/)
- Satura: 1M history Shorts views = $100 / $250 / $500 (low / base / high). — [Satura](https://saturaai.com/history-military-youtube-shorts-rpm)
- US Shorts RPM is $0.328 — [AIR](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-shorts-rpm-vs-long-form-how-much-do-shorts-earn-in-2026)

### Inferences (arithmetic on the cited RPMs)

| Monthly engaged views | $0.04 (low, global) | $0.10 (base) | $0.20 (good, Tier-1 heavy) | $0.33 (US-only, AIR) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1M | $40 | $100 | $200 | $330 |
| 10M | $400 | $1,000 | $2,000 | $3,300 |
| 30M | $1,200 | $3,000 | $6,000 | $9,900 |

- **Important:** from 1 Feb 2027, a channel under 10M engaged views in the trailing 90 days (about 3.33M a month) earns **$0 from the Shorts pool**. The 1M-a-month row therefore only exists **before** Feb 2027. After that it is zero unless the channel sustains 3.33M or more a month.
- One Short a day at 1M views a month means about 33k engaged views per Short. Holding the post-2027 floor needs about 111k per Short per day on average.
- Sources of revenue other than ads:
  - Memberships and Supers (500-sub tier)
  - Shopping affiliate
  - Brand deals
  - Long-form compilations, where education RPMs run 50-150x higher per view

### Gaps
- No verified data on Premium revenue as a share of Shorts income.

## Q6. How long new channels take to reach YPP (statistics)

### Takeaway
There is no rigorous public study of time-to-YPP. The best census data (CreatorDB, Aug 2026) shows only about **2.1%** of Shorts-posting channels with 1,000+ subs average 500k views per Short over their last 20 Shorts, a proxy for the 10M Shorts bar. Blogs give typical times of 6-24 months. Reaching it in one month is a top-percentile outcome.

### Cited Findings
- CreatorDB census, 12 Aug 2026:
  - 1,468,073 active channels with 1,000+ subs
  - 1,262,288 of them post Shorts; 25,975 (about 2.1%) have their last 20 Shorts totaling 10M+ views, and 12,819 reach 20M+
  - 51.0% of active 1k+ channels are below the old 4,000-hour bar
  - About 84,551 (5.8%, range about 50k-125k) sit between 4k and 8k hours, "stranded" by the new rule; education's stranded share is 6.4%

  This is a model-based estimate from public view data. — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/research/youtube-8000-hour-rule/)
- Blog and vendor estimates:
  - Most creators need 6-18 or 12-24 months to qualify
  - Individual creator anecdotes range from about 15 days to about 2.5 months
  - "Fewer than 10%" / "<6%" / "about 3%" of channels are ever monetized — all unsourced

  — [vidIQ](https://www.vidiq.com/blog/post/get-monetized-youtube); [AIR](https://air.io/en/monetization/how-long-does-it-take-to-get-monetized-on-youtube); [Milx](https://milx.app/en/guidelines/how-long-does-it-take-to-succeed-on-youtube)

### Inferences
- The 2.1% figure is measured among channels **already past 1,000 subs**. Among all new channels the share reaching 10M Shorts views in their first 90 days, let alone 30 days, is far smaller. Plan for 3-6+ months as the realistic case. Treat the 500-sub / 3M-view tier as the achievable 30-60-day milestone, and only if Shorts break out.
- Calendar for a start in mid-Nov 2026:
  - Threshold hit by about mid-Dec 2026
  - Review of about 1 month lands in mid-Jan 2027, before the 1 Feb rule change
  - Any later slip probably means the 20M bar (transition rules are unconfirmed)

### Gaps
- No vidIQ, TubeBuddy or Social Blade study gives a median time-to-YPP for new channels. The "% of channels monetized" figures are unsourced.
