# Cross-platform revenue for a new vertical cartoon history/science channel (2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-11-07. Context: one original 60-70 s vertical 3D cartoon story per day, AI voice, burned-in subtitles, original music, CC0 SFX, same master file could go to several platforms.

Source-quality warning that applies to the whole file: official pages from TikTok (support.tiktok.com redirect returned an empty shell), X (help.x.com, 403) and PetaPixel (403) could not be fetched. Most payout numbers below come from third-party creator/SEO blogs, and they disagree. Each number is marked with its source. Treat RPMs as directional, not audited.

## 1. TikTok Creator Rewards Program

### Takeaway
Requires 10k followers + 100k views in 30 days + 18+ + videos of at least 1 minute, and runs in only about 8 countries (US, UK, Germany, France, Japan, South Korea, Brazil, Mexico). A new account is very unlikely to reach 10k followers in 30 days, so it is a month-2-to-6 revenue path. Reported RPM is roughly $0.40-$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views. The 60-70 s length already clears the 1-minute rule, but it leaves little margin, so keep every cut above 61 s.

### Cited Findings
- Eligibility commonly reported: 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in the last 30 days, 18+, original videos of at least one minute — [Toptal, 2026](https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/how-to-join-the-tiktok-creator-rewards-program); [PostLink, 2026](https://postlinkapp.com/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-program); [CreatorsAgency, 2026](https://creatorsagency.co/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-2026)
- Eligible countries reported for 2026: United States, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Japan, South Korea, Brazil, Mexico. Mexico was added later (attributed to an October 2025 announcement), which is why older lists show seven. — [Quasa, 2026](https://quasa.io/media/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-eligible-countries-in-2026); [OneStream, 2026](https://onestream.live/blog/what-is-the-tiktok-creator-rewards-program/). TikTok publishes no single official country list. The practical check is whether "Creator Rewards Program" appears in TikTok Studio / Creator Tools for the account — [Multilogin, 2026](https://multilogin.com/blog/mobile/tiktok-creator-rewards-program/)
- Canada and Australia "may gain access in 2026" is speculation, not confirmed — same sources as above
- RPM: average reported $0.40-$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views. Up to ~$6 has been claimed for niche content, but that looks like an outlier. Viewer geography is the main driver (US views pay much more). — [TTS Vibes, 2026](https://insights.ttsvibes.com/tiktok-creator-fund-earnings-by-view-count/); [Elev8or, 2026](https://www.elev8or.io/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-explained)
- US program terms were reportedly updated on July 20, 2026, with no published summary of what changed — [ShortsFast, 2026](https://shortsfast.com/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-2026-requirements/)
- AI content, conflicting reports. Some blogs say fully AI-generated content is excluded from Creator Rewards and AI-assisted work is fine — [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/ai-generated-videos-monetised-tiktok/); [Storrito](https://storrito.com/resources/what-tiktoks-ai-monetization-restrictions-signal-for-creator-income/). Others say there is no blanket AI ban — [Clippie](https://clippie.ai/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-faceless-ai-videos); [ReelMind](https://reelmind.ai/blogs/can-you-make-money-posting-ai-videos-on-tiktok-and-youtube-shorts-in-2026-curren). Sources agree that realistic AI images, audio or video must be labeled with TikTok's AI-generated label.
- Unoriginal content is ineligible. That covers uploads without new creative edits, clips carrying another creator's watermark, and someone else's clip with only speed changes, filters, text or stickers added. One blog shows an "Ineligible: Unoriginal or synthetic content" status label. — [SocialBoostDigital](https://socialboostdigital.com/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-unoriginal-content-flag)

### Inferences
- Example: 1M qualified views in a month at $0.40-$1.00 RPM is about $400-$1,000. A new channel is more likely to see 0-200k views in month one, and it has no payout until it passes the 10k-follower gate.
- AI-voice risk: the visuals are original 3D animation, not realistic people, and the scripts are original stories. That puts the channel in the "AI-assisted, original" bucket rather than "fully synthetic". Use the AI-generated content label anyway, because labeling is required and cheap. Narration from a generic text-to-speech voice reading stock-like scripts is what creators report getting flagged, so a distinctive voice and personality help.
- If the owner lives outside the ~8 listed countries (for example Belgium, Switzerland, Canada, Morocco or Romania), Creator Rewards is simply unavailable. The country is set by account registration and residence, and using a VPN or someone else's identity breaks the terms.

### Gaps
- Official TikTok page text could not be retrieved (the support URL redirected to an empty JS shell). The exact qualified-view rules are not confirmed from TikTok itself. Commonly cited rules are 5+ seconds watched, For You feed only, no paid views, and a fraud filter, but I could not source these here.
- The owner's country is unknown.

## 2. Facebook / Meta and Instagram

### Takeaway
Facebook Content Monetization is the merged program. It covers Reels, long video, Stories, photos and text, pays on performance per qualified view, and is still invite-only with an interest form. The legacy in-stream, Ads on Reels and Performance bonus programs ended on August 31, 2025. Reels RPM is reported anywhere from $0.02 to about $1.20 per 1,000 views. Instagram has no broadly available Reels payout in 2026; the old Reels Play bonus closed in 2023. Creator Fast Track needs 100k+ followers elsewhere, so it does not apply to a new channel.

### Cited Findings
- Meta newsroom, March 18-19, 2026: Facebook Content Monetization is invite-only. Creators "express interest" in Professional Dashboard → Monetization → Content Monetization. It pays on performance, with "added emphasis on original content creators". It introduces the metrics Qualified Views, Earnings Rate (approximate earnings per 1,000 qualified views) and Non-Qualified Views. — [Meta Newsroom, 2026-03-18](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/creator-fast-track-grow-your-audience-earn-money-on-facebook/)
- Creator Fast Track pays a guaranteed $1,000/month (100k+ followers on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube) or $3,000/month (1M+) for 3 months, plus immediate Content Monetization access — [Meta Newsroom, 2026-03-18](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/creator-fast-track-grow-your-audience-earn-money-on-facebook/). A blog adds a posting condition of 15 eligible Reels a month on 10+ separate days — [StreamClipping](https://streamclipping.ai/blog/facebook-reels-monetization). Meta's post does not state that condition.
- Facebook paid creators nearly $3B in 2025 (+35% year over year), about 60% of it for Reels — [Meta Newsroom, 2026-03-18](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/creator-fast-track-grow-your-audience-earn-money-on-facebook/)
- Requirements reported: 18+, a Page or professional-mode profile, a supported country and language, policy compliance, original content, and Reels at least 10 s long. No public follower minimum. — [CreatorsAgency, 2026](https://creatorsagency.co/blog/facebook-content-monetization-requirements-2026); [Clip-Short](https://www.clip-short.com/guides/facebook-content-monetization)
- Legacy in-stream, Ads on Reels and Performance bonus ended August 31, 2025 — [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/monetize-facebook-reels/)
- Supported countries are reported as ~70 countries and territories as of October 2026, unverified — [Brillay](https://brillay.com/resources/facebook-monetization-requirements)
- Reels RPM, conflicting. "Most creators" earn $0.02-$0.20 per 1,000 views — [PostPlanify](https://postplanify.com/blog/how-much-does-facebook-reels-pay-per-1000-views). Others report $0.30-$1.20 per 1,000 with US/UK-heavy audiences at the top — [AdaptlyPost, 2026](https://adaptlypost.com/blog/how-much-does-facebook-reels-pay-per-1000-views). Another blog claims $1.50-$6 per 1,000 in Western Europe — [Fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/facebook-reels-monetization-earnings-2026-v2). The creator's own Earnings Rate in the dashboard is the only reliable figure.
- Instagram: the Reels Play Bonus closed to new invites in March 2023. Any 2026 "Performance Bonus" is described as invite-only and undocumented, and sources disagree on whether it exists. — [Fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/instagram-reels-play-bonus-status-2026); [TimeToPost](https://timetopost.co/blog/instagram-reels-bonus-program-2026/); [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/does-instagram-pay-creators-for-reels/). There is a June 2026 report of Instagram paying "up to $10k bonus" to creators for posting Reels — [Telangana Today, 2026-06-24](https://telanganatoday.com/instagram-paying-up-to-10k-bonus-to-creators-for-posting-reels). I did not verify this against Meta, and it is likely aimed at recruiting established creators.
- Instagram Creator Marketplace (brand deals) is reportedly available at 10,000+ followers — [Fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/how-much-instagram-pays-for-reels)

### Inferences
- Facebook is the most realistic within-30-days platform payout for a new channel. It has no follower floor, the interest form can be filed on day 1, and Meta is actively recruiting original Reels creators. An invite is not guaranteed and the timing is unknown.
- Cross-posting a Reel to Facebook from Instagram (Accounts Center) is fine because it is the creator's own file. Meta's "original content" emphasis penalizes reposting others' content, not your own.
- Instagram is for audience and brand deals, not platform payout.

### Gaps
- No official Meta per-Reel rate. Every RPM figure is from third-party blogs.
- Meta does not publish the qualified-view definition for Facebook Content Monetization in the fetched page.
- Meta's official supported-country list was not retrieved.

## 3. Snapchat, X and other platforms

### Takeaway
Snapchat's Unified Monetization Program (from February 1, 2025) is invite-only and needs 50k followers, so it is out of reach in 30 days. X pays only Premium subscribers with large verified-impression counts. Neither is a 30-day path.

### Cited Findings
- Snapchat Unified Monetization Program launched February 1, 2025. Requirements: 50,000+ followers, 25+ posts a month to Saved Stories or Spotlight, and 12k hours of view time in the preceding 28 days. Search snippets confirmed those three. The extra alternatives sometimes quoted (posting on 10 of 28 days, or 10M snap views / 1M Spotlight views) come from my background knowledge of the announcement and are not verified here. Invitation-based. Monetizes ads inside Spotlight videos longer than 1 minute. — [Snap Newsroom, 2024-12](https://newsroom.snap.com/snapchat-new-creator-monetization); [TechCrunch, 2024-12-16](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/16/snapchat-introduces-a-unified-monetization-program-for-creators); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/snapchat-launches-streamlined-creator-monetization-process/735668/). Spotlight Rewards (the old per-view fund) was shut down — [Tubefilter, 2024-12-16](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/12/16/snapchat-new-monetization-program-shutting-down-spotlight-rewards/amp/)
- Spotlight payout reported at $0.10-$0.30 per 1,000 views in 2026, from a low-authority, anecdotal source — [Influencers Gone Wild](https://influencers-gone-wild.it.com/?p=311)
- X now runs an "Original Content Rewards Program" (help page found in search: [help.x.com](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards)). The page returned 403, so its terms are unverified. Historically X creator revenue required X Premium, 500+ verified followers and 5M organic impressions in 3 months. That comes from my background knowledge, not a source fetched here, so the writer should treat it as unverified.

### Inferences
- Snapchat: post anyway as a low-effort extra channel, since the files already fit 9:16 and exceed 1 minute. Revenue only starts after 50k followers.
- Moj, Zapp and similar India-centric apps are only relevant if the audience is Indian. I did not research them because of the tool-call budget.

### Gaps
- Current X Original Content Rewards terms and payouts (page blocked).
- Moj, Zapp, Triller and other paying short-video apps were not researched.

## 4. Does cross-posting the same video hurt reach? Instagram originality rules

### Takeaway
Posting your own clean master file (no other app's watermark) on several platforms is not penalized by any platform's stated rules. What gets penalized is another app's watermark (TikTok logo on Reels or Shorts) and reposting content you did not create. Export a clean file per platform from the render, and never download from TikTok and re-upload.

### Cited Findings
- Instagram has downranked Reels with visible logos or watermarks from other apps since 2021. Content edited outside Instagram and then uploaded is not penalized. Mosseri: "We build classifiers to predict how *likely* something is to be original." — [Social Media Today, 2022](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagrams-updating-its-ranking-algorithm-to-put-more-focus-on-original-co/622424); [PetaPixel, 2022-04-21](https://petapixel.com/2022/04/21/instagram-tweaks-its-algorithm-to-value-original-content-more)
- In 2024 Instagram began replacing reposts with the original video in recommendations (crediting the original creator), and serial aggregators that don't enhance content were removed from recommendations — [Tubefilter, 2024-04-30](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/04/30/instagram-meta-threads-original-content-algorithm-bonus-program-monetization/amp/)
- In late April 2026 Instagram extended the originality rule from Reels to photos and carousels. Accounts that mainly share content they didn't create or meaningfully transform are no longer recommended to non-followers. A watermark, border, crop or caption does not make content "original". — [PetaPixel, 2026-04-30](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/) (seen via search snippet, page 403); [Coruzant](https://coruzant.com/digital-strategy/instagram-originality-rule/)
- Unverified blog claims: a "10+ reposts in 30 days" threshold — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers); "60-70% less reach" for watermarked posts — [Likes.io](https://likes.io/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026). Neither is traced to Meta.
- YouTube adds its own watermark to Shorts downloaded from YouTube Studio as an anti-theft measure — [Adgully](https://www.adgully.com/youtube-adds-watermarks-to-stem-stealing-of-short-videos-121670.html?amp=1). Use the original render, not a Studio download, when posting elsewhere.
- "No platform has said it punishes you for posting your own content on other apps too." Platforms penalize unoriginal content and posts carrying another app's watermark. — [Post Bridge](https://www.post-bridge.com/blog/does-cross-posting-hurt-engagement)
- Vendor case study (single example, not controlled): the same TikTok posted to YouTube Shorts averaged 450 views with the TikTok watermark versus 12,000 as a clean master — [PostEverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/how-to-remove-tiktok-watermark-before-posting) citing Joyspace
- TikTok treats content carrying another creator's watermark or imported without new edits as unoriginal, which makes it ineligible for Creator Rewards — [SocialBoostDigital](https://socialboostdigital.com/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-unoriginal-content-flag)
- YouTube "inauthentic content" (renamed from "repetitious content"), enforced from July 15, 2025, targets mass-produced or repetitive content with only slight variations. It is enforced at channel level ("monetization may be removed from your entire channel"). YouTube's Rene Ritchie called it a "minor update". AI is not named. — [Search Engine Journal, 2025-07](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-clarifies-monetization-update-targeting-spam-not-reaction-channels/550755/); [Social Media Today, 2025-07](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-clarifies-monetization-update-inauthentic-repeated-content/752892/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/youtube-updates-monetisation-policies-ai-and-repetitive-content-ban-begins-july-15-1.500192660)

### Inferences
- For this channel: export the clean MP4 from the render pipeline and upload natively to each app. Don't use another app's downloaded file. Use the native caption, hashtag and cover per platform.
- AI-voice flag: YouTube's inauthentic-content rule is the main AI-channel risk, and because it is enforced per channel, a daily template-like series is exactly what reviewers look at. Each video telling a distinct story with original animation and a varied structure is the defense. Avoid identical intros, the same voice cadence and identical shot patterns across videos.
- Posting the same day on all platforms is fine. Whether to stagger is a strategy choice with no evidence either way.

### Gaps
- No platform-published or independent controlled study quantifies cross-post reach loss.
- No official TikTok statement on penalizing content first posted on YouTube or Instagram.

## 5. Revenue before YPP: brand deals, affiliates, Patreon, licensing, services

### Takeaway
Brand deals pay first, but usually only after roughly 1k-10k engaged followers. Nano rates are about $20-$250 per post on Instagram, $5-$125 on TikTok and $20-$1,000 on YouTube. Education is one of the higher-CPM niches. Affiliate links and Patreon can be set up on day 1 but earn little at small scale. Making videos as a service for others is the only path that can pay meaningful money within 30 days, because it does not depend on audience size. Licensing AI animation on stock sites is constrained: real-event titles and AI content flags trigger rejections.

### Cited Findings
- Nano Instagram $50-$200 per post, nano TikTok $25-$125, micro YouTube $300-$1,000 per video — [Heepsy, 2025](https://blog.heepsy.com/posts/how-much-do-influencers-make-in-2025)
- Nano Instagram $20-$100, nano TikTok $5-$50, nano YouTube $20-$200 per video — [Meltwater](https://meltwater.com/en/blog/influencer-marketing-costs-rates-pricing)
- Micro Instagram roughly $150-$300 at the low end and $300-$500+ mid-range — [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/micro-influencer-rates/)
- 2026: nano Instagram $50-$250, micro $250-$1,500; YouTube pays the most per post — [Elev8or Creator Rate Report 2026](https://www.elev8or.io/creator-rate-report-2026)
- Education is among the highest-CPM niches, roughly $2-$15 depending on niche — [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/micro-influencer-rates/); education is listed among the best-performing niches — [InfluenceFlow, 2025](https://influenceflow.io/resources/influencer-marketing-benchmarks-and-industry-comparisons-2025)
- YouTube BrandConnect official requirements: 18+, in YPP and eligible for ad and Premium revenue sharing — [Google Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9385307). A rename to "Creator Partnerships" is reported but unverified — [Ainfluencer](https://ainfluencer.com/youtube-brandconnect/). So BrandConnect is not available before full YPP.
- Passionfroot is a B2B creator-sponsorship storefront with no published platform minimum. It reportedly raised $15M in July 2026 to expand in the US. — [Passionfroot creators](https://www.passionfroot.me/creators); [Rutab, 2026-07-22](https://rutab.net/b/hardware/2026/07/22/passionfroot-privlek-15-mln-dlya-rasshireniya-b2b-marketpleysa-kreatorov-v-ssha.html); [TechCrunch, 2024-10-21](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/21/passionfroot-is-building-a-platform-for-brand-and-creator-colloboration-with-a-focus-on-b2b-sector)
- vidIQ lists 16 brand-deal matchmaking companies for YouTubers (2026) — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/companies-matching-youtubers-with-brand-deals/)
- Adobe Stock accepts generative-AI video only if flagged as AI at submission, with no embedded text or watermarks, no imitation of other artists' styles and no prompts tied to real IP. Identifiable people need a model release. Contributors earn royalties under the standard royalty guidelines. — [Adobe HelpX](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-generative-ai-content/generative-ai-video-submission-guidelines.html). Community reports describe a clip removed because its title implied a real historic event, and near-total rejection of AI videos over anomalies such as hands — [Adobe Community](https://community.adobe.com/questions-38/audit-removal-incompatible-with-terms-317183); [Adobe Community](https://community.adobe.com/questions-38/ai-generated-stock-videos-are-being-rejected-almost-100-1560557)

### Inferences
- Burned-in subtitles would violate Adobe Stock's "no embedded text" rule. Stock licensing needs clean, caption-free renders of individual shots, which the pipeline can export. Clips about real history events may also trip Adobe's real-event rule. Licensing to education publishers directly (clean versions without captions) is plausible but has no sourced rates.
- Services (custom explainer shorts for museums, edtech, teachers' YouTube channels, small brands) are the fastest cash. The pipeline already produces a finished 60-70 s short a day. I found no sourced price benchmarks in this session.
- Affiliates (Amazon Associates for history books, Audible) can be added to bios and descriptions on day 1. Amazon Associates historically needs 3 qualifying sales in the first 180 days to stay approved (background knowledge, unverified here). Expect only a few dollars a month at small scale.
- Patreon or Ko-fi can be set up immediately, but at under 1k followers it typically yields near zero (unsourced).

### Gaps
- No education-niche-specific per-post rates and no data on when brands start contacting history/science shorts channels.
- Amazon Associates and Audible bounty terms for 2026 were not fetched.
- No price data for done-for-you short-video services, and no licensing rates for education publishers.
- Pond5 and Shutterstock AI-video policies were not found.

## 6. YouTube lower YPP tier fan funding (Super Thanks, memberships)

### Takeaway
The expanded tier opens at 500 subscribers + 3 public uploads in 90 days + 3,000 watch hours in 12 months or 3M Shorts views in 90 days. It unlocks Super Thanks, Super Chat, Super Stickers, memberships and the merch shelf, but no ad revenue. Small channels report earning very little from these (the only figure found is an unverified $10-$100/month).

### Cited Findings
- Expanded tier: 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in the last 90 days, and either 3,000 watch hours in the past year or 3M Shorts views in 90 days. Initially available in the US, UK, Canada, Taiwan and South Korea, with more countries later. — [MediaPost, 2023-06](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/386299/youtube-expands-eligibility-to-its-partner-and-sho.html)
- The tier unlocks Super Thanks, channel memberships, Super Chat and the merch shelf, not ad revenue. The ad-revenue tier needs 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours or 10M Shorts views in 90 days. — [Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/apps/youtube-partner-program-eligibility-requirements); [Fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-partner-program-500-subscribers)
- Claimed: full-tier thresholds rise for new applicants on February 1, 2027 (8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views). Unverified against YouTube help, so flag it for checking. — [Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/apps/youtube-partner-program-eligibility-requirements)
- Typical income at this level is "$10-$100 per month" (single-blog estimate, unverified) — [Fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-partner-program-500-subscribers)

### Inferences
- With daily 60-70 s Shorts, 3M Shorts views in 90 days is the realistic path to the lower tier. Watch hours are hard for Shorts. Super Thanks on Shorts is small money and mainly matters as a signal of fan loyalty.
- Shorts fan-funding revenue is tiny compared with what brand deals pay at the same audience size.

### Gaps
- No creator-reported Super Thanks or membership earnings data for small Shorts channels was found.
- The 2027 threshold change could not be confirmed from YouTube.
